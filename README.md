# My Favorite Series: *My Hero Academia* (僕のヒーローアカデミア)

## Overview
**My Hero Academia** is a Japanese superhero anime based on the manga by **Kōhei Horikoshi**.  
It’s set in a world where most people have superpowers called **“Quirks.”**  
The story follows **Izuku Midoriya**, a boy born without a Quirk, who still dreams of becoming the greatest hero.

> *“When you have to save someone, they’re usually in a scary situation. A true hero saves them anyway!”*  
> — Izuku Midoriya

---

## Plot Summary
- In a world where 80% of people have superpowers, heroes protect society from villains.  
- **Deku**, born without a Quirk, meets his idol **All Might**, who passes his power **One For All** to him.  
- At **U.A. High School**, Deku trains to become the next Symbol of Peace while facing dangerous enemies and personal challenges.  

---

## Why I Love It
- Inspiring message about never giving up on your dreams  
- Amazing hero battles and emotional moments  
- Strong character development  
- Motivating soundtrack and great animation by **Bones Studio**
